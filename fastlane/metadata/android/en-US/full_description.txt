ColAI is a multi-account AI workspace on Android that lets you use multiple accounts on web-based AI services without constantly logging in and out.

If you have separate work, personal, and project accounts on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, or Perplexity, standard mobile browsers force you to switch accounts or use multiple separate apps. ColAI solves this by running each session in its own isolated GeckoView container.

FEATURES:

• Isolated Session Containers
Each session runs in a separate isolated container context with its own cookies, local storage, and cache. You can stay logged into multiple accounts on the same AI service at the same time without cross-account tracking or session resets.

• Bento Grid Dashboard
Manage all your AI services from a clean home screen. Comes pre-configured for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok, and Perplexity. You can also add custom web services.

• Custom Services with Logo Editor
Add any web AI tool by entering its URL. Choose an icon from your device or fetch one from a link, then crop and rotate it before saving.

• Biometric and PIN Protection
Lock individual sessions behind your fingerprint or a 4-digit PIN. A master recovery key is generated when you set up your PIN so you can regain access if needed.

• Home Screen Widgets
Add widgets to your Android home screen to launch directly into a specific session of your chosen AI service, or start voice input quickly.

• Privacy and Tracking Protection
No analytics, no ads, and no background tracking servers. Sends Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) headers with requests, and blocks common "Install Mobile App" banners.

• Per-Session Storage Controls
Clear cookies or cached data for a single account without touching your other sessions.

• Encrypted Backups
Export your workspace configuration and saved services to an AES-256-GCM encrypted backup file protected by your password.

• Clean Native Android Interface
Built natively with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose with responsive layouts, haptic feedback, and support for system light and dark themes.
